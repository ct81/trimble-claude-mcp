RoachHarbor

Connected workflows for architecture, engineering, and construction.

RoachHarbor is a web application (also known as Trimble Claude MCP) with a visual Workflow Builder. You use it to bring project files, spreadsheets, and Trimble Connect data into one workflow: import information, process it, call an API or MCP tool, and display or export the result.

RoachHarbor is developed and operated by an independent developer. Support and privacy contact: noodletan@outlook.com.

This page is public. You do not need to sign in to read it, to read the Privacy Policy, or to see what data the app requests.

What RoachHarbor Does

Files and results

Import PDF, Excel, CSV, IFC, and DXF files. Use connected extraction or conversion tools, inspect results in grid or form views, and export supported results to Excel or CSV.

Google Sheets import

Sign in with your own Google account, pick one of your spreadsheets and a sheet inside it, and import its rows into a workflow for display, processing, or export.

Trimble Connect

Sign in with Trimble to use project, file, model, organizer, property-set, topic, and Status Sharing operations through MCP tools.

APIs and local bridges

Connect REST APIs and MCP services to workflow data. Tekla, Trimble Structural Designer, and SketchUp integrations need the matching bridge or service running on your own workstation; cloud hosting alone cannot reach software on your computer.

Optional Claude AI

Ask Claude questions or connect a Claude component to analyze workflow input. This requires a server-side Anthropic integration. Review generated answers before using them for project decisions.

Saved workflows and messaging

Save named workflows for reuse. Scheduled workflows run in your browser while the page is open. WhatsApp and Telegram messaging are available only when configured by the operator and authorized by the relevant account.

Why RoachHarbor Requests Your Google Data

Google access is used for one purpose: letting you import rows from your own Google Sheets into a workflow. It is requested only when you choose the Google Sheets import component and click to connect. Google handles sign-in, and RoachHarbor never sees or asks for your Google password.

Permission requestedWhat it lets RoachHarbor doWhy it is needed
View your Google Drive files (read-only)
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly		 List the names and IDs of your spreadsheets so you can choose one. You pick the spreadsheet from a list inside the app instead of pasting an ID. Only spreadsheet names and IDs are used for the picker.
View your Google Sheets spreadsheets (read-only)
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly		 Read the sheet names and the rows of the spreadsheet you selected. These rows are the input to your workflow.
  • RoachHarbor does not request permission to edit, create, share, or delete any of your Google files.
  • Only the spreadsheet and sheet you select are read. Other Drive files are not opened.
  • Google authorization tokens are kept in your browser's memory and are not stored on our server. Closing the page ends the session.
  • The selected spreadsheet ID and sheet name can be saved inside a workflow if you save it.
  • We do not sell your Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • Imported rows are shown to you and can be exported by you. If you connect them to Claude, MCP tools, or REST API components, running the workflow sends that data to the destination you chose. Review the workflow before running it.

RoachHarbor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access any time: open your Google Account permissions, select RoachHarbor, and choose Remove access.

Other Data the App Handles

  • Trimble sign-in: supplies your account details, project information, and authorization tokens so RoachHarbor can perform the project operations you authorize.
  • Imported files: PDF, Excel, CSV, IFC, and DXF files are processed only to produce the extraction, conversion, analysis, or export you request.
  • Claude components: your prompt and the workflow data you connect are sent to our server and to Anthropic to generate an answer.
  • Saved workflows: names, settings, schedules, and optionally uploaded files are stored in your browser and, if you save to the server, in Firebase Realtime Database or on the server's disk.
